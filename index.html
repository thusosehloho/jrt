Who we are

A multidisciplinary team with a shared focus.

JRT Security brings together technical depth, human-centred research and commercial experience. Our members have worked across different industries and disciplines, but share a belief that understanding context and people is essential to tackling difficult cybersecurity problems.

As part of Cyber Innovate, we are developing our understanding of cybersecurity challenges, with particular interest in operational technology (OT), industrial systems and the organisations that depend on them.

Organisational perspectiveBusiness transformation, consulting, strategy and the wider context of technology decisions.

Part of the Cyber Innovate community

JRT Security is a team of scholars participating in Cyber Innovate, a postgraduate cybersecurity innovation programme at Munster Technological University in Cork, Ireland. The programme brings experienced professionals together from technical, design and commercial backgrounds.
